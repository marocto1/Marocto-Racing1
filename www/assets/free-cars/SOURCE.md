# Kenney Car Kit — bundled car bodies

Source: Kenney Car Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit)
Author: Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal

The OBJ files in this directory are unmodified geometry from Kenney Car Kit.
They were mirrored from a repository copy that preserves the original Kenney headers and license.
The original colormap texture is intentionally not bundled in this milestone; Marocto Racing applies its own per-car tint/material shader.
